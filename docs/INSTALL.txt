OpenInsert 0.1.0 — macOS 13 or later

1. Drag OpenInsert.app to Applications.
2. Open it and configure your own Gemini API key in Connection & Permissions.
3. Enable microphone and Accessibility permission for OpenInsert.
4. Agree to sending your actively recorded audio and vocabulary directly to Google.
5. Quit other apps that use Option + Space. Focus a text field.
6. Hold Option + Space, speak, and release. Or tap once to start and again to stop.

This community build is ad-hoc signed, not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized.
macOS may block a downloaded build. Inspect the source or build locally if needed.
Do not disable Gatekeeper. See Apple's official instructions if you choose to open it:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Automatic insertion does not work in every app. It never presses Return.
Results remain available for manual copy if the destination changes or refuses input.
Gemini usage, pricing, and data processing follow your Google account's terms.
